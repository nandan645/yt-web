YouTube cookies

If YouTube says “Sign in to confirm you’re not a bot”, upload a cookies.txt from a logged-in browser. Every download will use it.

Checking…
  • Use a private/incognito window: log in to YouTube, open youtube.com/robots.txt, export cookies with a cookies.txt extension, then close the window (this keeps the cookies from being rotated).
  • A spare Google account is safer — accounts used with downloaders can get flagged.
  • Stored only on this machine, readable only by you, and never sent back to the page. yt-dlp guide
